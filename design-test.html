Roots

Mudit grew up in Lucknow with values shaped by his parents—being grounded, staying close to people, and serving the community. These principles continue to guide his work today.

Education

He completed his schooling at The Indian Public School in Dehradun before moving to the United States to study at Arizona State University.

Service at ASU

Beyond academics, Mudit dedicated time to community and mentorship. He was the Founding First Officer of PIERA, served as a residential peer mentor, and worked at ISSC.

Current Work

Today, Mudit leads a team across his ventures—Devalok and Oshm—while establishing an office in Lucknow.

Recognition

In December 2025, Mudit was selected as the Convocation Speaker and Impact Award recipient for the Fulton Schools of Engineering at ASU. He has been featured in The Chronicle of Higher Education, ASU News, and QS Insights Magazine.
