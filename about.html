Roots

Mudit Lal is a founder, engineer and designer from Lucknow, Bharat. His interest in robotics began at thirteen, and he grew up with values shaped by his parents—being grounded, staying close to people, and serving the community. These principles, rooted in Dharma, continue to guide his work.

Education

After schooling at The Indian Public SchoolResidential school in Dehradun in Dehradun, Mudit moved to the United States to study at Arizona State University. At ASU's Polytechnic CampusTechnology-focused campus in Mesa, AZ, he is completing dual degrees—a B.S. in Engineering (Robotics) and a B.S. in TEMTechnological Entrepreneurship and Management—graduating in December 2025.

Service at ASU

As Founding First Officer of PIERA, he built an organization hosting an annual Policy Symposium"Shark Tank, but for policy" connecting students with Indian government officials. He also served as Director of Outreach for USG PolytechnicUndergraduate Student Government, a residential peer mentor, and an International Relations Assistant at the ISSCInternational Students and Scholars Center.

Ventures

Mudit leads a 12-person team at Devalok, a design studio featured in Packaging of the World and World Brand Design Society. He is also building Oshm for quick-commerce infrastructure and BrahmalokAutomation-as-a-Service for MSMEs for robotics automation.

Recognition

Mudit has been selected as the Convocation Speaker and Impact Award recipient for the Fulton Schools of EngineeringASU's engineering college. Featured in The Chronicle of Higher Education, ASU News, and QS Insights Magazine. After graduation, he will return to Lucknow to continue building his ventures from home.
